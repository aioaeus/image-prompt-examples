## 修改說明 / How to Adapt

- 換布景：把「彩繪花園」換成海灘、雪山或太空，機器人撐起的「假象」主題不變。
- 換人物：把情侶換成一家三口或生日派對的孩子，CHARACTER 段同步改年齡服裝。
- 改結局感：把布景寫成正在被風吹落一角，露出更多廢墟，浪漫與殘酷的對比更強。

- Swap the backdrop: replace the painted garden with a beach, snowy mountains or space — the "illusion" theme stays.
- Change the cast: swap the couple for a family of three or birthday-party kids, updating ages and outfits in CHARACTER.
- Sharpen the ending: show one corner of the backdrop peeling in the wind, revealing more ruins, for a starker romance-versus-ruin contrast.
