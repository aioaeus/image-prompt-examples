# 案例 89：Insight 品牌標誌 | Case 89: Insight See the Pattern Logo

![效果圖 / Result](image.png)

極簡扁平標誌設計：三個錯落堆疊的青色圓角矩形，配一枚圓點，象徵層層數據匯聚成焦點；下方粗體「Insight」字標與淺色標語「See the Pattern」，乾淨利落的品牌識別。

Minimal flat logo design: three stacked cyan rounded rectangles offset like steps, plus a small circle — a stylised attention window where layered data converges into a focused core — above a bold "Insight" wordmark and the light slogan "See the Pattern".

## 完整提示詞 / Full Prompt

```
LOGO CONCEPT: A stylized attention window conveying layered data processing converging into a focused core.

SYMBOL & GEOMETRY: Three stacked open rounded rectangles (width 80 px, height 20 px) offset right by 20 px each. A small circle (radius 6 px) sits 30 px to the right of the rightmost rectangle, centered vertically. Rectangles and circle are filled #00AEEF. The circle is separated by a 10 px gap. All shapes have 2 px inner bevel to suggest depth.

SURFACE & DEPTH: Flat, no bevel. All shapes solid.

LETTERING: Bold 48 px for wordmark, light 24 px for slogan.

COLOR: #00AEEF (teal) for shapes; background #FFFFFF. Gradient disabled; flat colors.

COMPOSITION: Icon centered above wordmark; 10 px margin icon‑name, 5 px margin name‑slogan. Wordmark in bold sans-serif, 48 px. Slogan in light sans-serif, 24 px.

EXACT LOGO TEXT: "Insight".

SLOGAN: Render verbatim "See the Pattern".

SLOGAN TYPOGRAPHY: Light 24 px, tracking +0.5, color #555555, centered under wordmark
```

## 適用模型 / Recommended Models

擅長標誌與向量風格的模型：GPT Image（Image2）、Ideogram、Recraft。

Models strong at logos and vector style: GPT Image (Image2), Ideogram, Recraft.

## 修改說明 / How to Adapt

- 換品牌名：把 "Insight" 與 "See the Pattern" 換成自家名稱與標語，EXACT LOGO TEXT 與 SLOGAN 兩段同步改。
- 換配色：#00AEEF 青色換成品牌主色，COLOR 段的形狀色與字標色整組換。
- 換圖形：三個圓角矩形換成其他幾何組合，SYMBOL & GEOMETRY 段的尺寸與偏移量重寫。

- Change the brand name: swap "Insight" and "See the Pattern" for your own name and slogan; update EXACT LOGO TEXT and SLOGAN together.
- Change the palette: swap the #00AEEF teal for your brand colour; replace the shape and wordmark colours in COLOR as a set.
- Change the symbol: swap the three rounded rectangles for another geometric set; rewrite the sizes and offsets in SYMBOL & GEOMETRY.
