# 案例 03：復古旗袍數碼相機人像 | Case 03: Vintage Qipao Flash-Photography Portrait

![效果圖 / Result](image.png)

懷舊早期數碼相機感：直閃、暖白牆、霧藍灰花卉旗袍，像私人相冊裡的真實照片而不是廣告大片。

Nostalgic early-digital-camera look: direct on-camera flash, warm off-white wall, dusty blue-grey floral qipao — like a real photo from a personal album, not a commercial campaign.

## 完整提示詞 / Full Prompt

```
Ultra-realistic candid portrait of a young adult East Asian woman photographed indoors with a nostalgic early-digital-camera aesthetic. She stands against a simple warm off-white wall, looking slightly downward and toward the camera with a calm, soft, almost dreamy expression.
She has long, glossy black hair styled into an elegant traditional-inspired updo, with the hair gathered high at the back and secured with simple dark hair sticks. Several loose curled strands frame both sides of her face, while wispy flyaways add natural imperfection.
Her makeup is refined and feminine: softly defined brows, subtle warm-brown eye makeup, delicate eyeliner, naturally flushed cheeks, and glossy muted-rose lips. Keep the skin realistic with visible natural texture rather than an overly airbrushed finish.
Outfit
She wears a fitted vintage-inspired floral qipao / cheongsam in muted dusty blue-gray.
The dress features:
delicate Mandarin collar
short fitted sleeves
traditional diagonal front closure
subtle decorative frog-button detailing
fitted waist and elegant silhouette
soft satin-like fabric with realistic highlights
large multicolored floral print
The floral pattern should include peonies, roses, and smaller blossoms in muted:
dusty pink
burgundy
cream
pale yellow
sage green
muted coral
The neckline has a modest curved opening framed by the traditional collar construction.
The garment should look like a beautifully made vintage-inspired Chinese dress rather than a costume.
Pose
She stands naturally with both arms relaxed alongside her body.
Her shoulders are slightly angled rather than perfectly square to the camera.
Her head tilts subtly downward and to one side.
Her eyes look toward the camera with a quiet, composed expression.
Avoid an exaggerated smile—the mood should be soft, elegant, slightly mysterious, and introspective.
Hair Details
The hairstyle is an important focal point.
Create:
voluminous black hair
traditional-inspired high bun
two simple dark hair sticks extending horizontally
loose curled tendrils around the cheeks
slightly messy wisps around the crown
realistic individual strands
subtle shine from the flash
The hairstyle should combine classic East Asian elegance with a slightly imperfect personal-photo feeling.
Lighting
Use direct on-camera flash in a dim indoor environment.
The flash should produce:
softly illuminated face
realistic catchlights in the eyes
gentle highlights on the satin dress
subtle shadow behind her body
darker falloff around the edges
slightly cool flash against a warmer beige background
Avoid polished studio lighting.
The lighting should resemble an authentic compact-camera photograph taken indoors at night.
Photography Style
Nostalgic early-2000s / early-digital-camera portrait:
35mm equivalent lens
vertical portrait
direct flash
slight wide-angle perspective
subtle digital noise
mild highlight bloom
gentle lens softness
realistic skin texture
slightly muted colors
modest chromatic aberration
imperfect exposure
candid personal-photo aesthetic
The image should look like a real photograph from someone's personal photo archive, not a commercial fashion campaign.
Composition
Vertical 4:5 portrait.
Frame her from approximately the hips upward.
Place her slightly off-center.
Leave generous negative space around her head and shoulders.
Keep the plain wall visible so that the colorful floral dress and dark hairstyle become the primary visual elements.
Use a slightly low-quality flash-camera feel while retaining high-resolution anatomical and fabric detail.
Color Palette
dusty blue-gray
muted burgundy
faded rose pink
cream
sage green
pale yellow
deep black hair
warm ivory background
Overall mood: elegant, nostalgic, feminine, traditional-inspired, quiet, romantic, slightly mysterious.
```

## 適用模型 / Recommended Models

適用於寫實人像模型：GPT Image（Image2）、Flux Pro、即夢寫實模式。重點是保留皮膚紋理與閃光質感，避免過度磨皮的模型設定。

Best on photorealistic portrait models: GPT Image (Image2), Flux Pro, Jimeng realistic mode. Keep skin texture and flash character; avoid over-smoothing settings.

## 修改說明 / How to Adapt

- 換旗袍花色：改 Outfit 段的花卉顏色清單（如改成墨綠底＋白梅），其他段落不用動。
- 換氛圍：把 Lighting 段的「direct on-camera flash」換成「window light at dusk」，整組就從夜晚閃光照變成黃昏自然光。
- 避免 AI 味：保留 Photography Style 段的 `subtle digital noise`、`imperfect exposure` 等「不完美」詞，是這組提示詞的關鍵。

- Change the dress print: edit the colour list in the Outfit section (e.g. dark-green base with white plum blossoms) and leave the rest untouched.
- Change the mood: replace `direct on-camera flash` in the Lighting section with `window light at dusk` to turn it into a natural-light portrait.
- Avoid the AI look: keep the "imperfections" in the Photography Style section (`subtle digital noise`, `imperfect exposure`) — they are the key to this prompt.
