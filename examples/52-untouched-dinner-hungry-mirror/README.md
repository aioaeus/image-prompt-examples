- 換對照組：把 SUBJECT & IDEA 的「富人＋飢餓倒影」換成新娘與鏡中老去的自己、或運動員與鏡中受傷的自己，主題隨之改變。

- 改光影：把餐桌寫成燭光暖調、鏡中世界寫成冷調，兩個世界的反差更強烈。

- Swap the pairing: replace the SUBJECT & IDEA line's wealthy diner and starving reflection with a bride and her aged self, or an athlete and his injured self — the theme shifts with it.
- Change the mirror: swap the mirror for a floor-to-ceiling window reflection or a phone selfie camera, closer to modern life.
- Reshape the light: render the table in warm candlelight and the mirror world in cold tones for a stronger two-world contrast.
