中世紀泥金彩飾風：鎧甲騎士與小蝸牛在伊茲尼克瓷磚牆前對峙，蝸牛留下的虹彩黏液光痕與騎士的影子交織，莊嚴中透著一絲幽默。

Medieval marginalia style: an armored knight faces a humble snail before an Iznik tile wall, the snail's iridescent slime trail glowing against the knight's shadow — solemn, with a touch of humour.

## 完整提示詞 / Full Prompt

```
SUBJECT: Knight vs Snail Marginalia

SCENE: In a quiet courtyard bathed in soft side light, a armored knight faces a humble snail on a wall of white glazed tiles. The snail, surprisingly, leaves a luminous trail of iridescent slime that glows against the interlocking tulip motifs, creating a subtle halo. The knight's polished armor catches a faint reflection of the tiles, its shadow stretching over the snail, a moment of unexpected harmony.

STYLE & LIGHT: Iznik tilework with glazed white tiles featuring interlocking tulip motifs.

CAMERA: Wide-angle overhead shot capturing the knight, snail, tile pattern, reflections, and shadows.

WATERMARK: Add the exact text "aioae.us" in the bottom-right corner at 90% transparency (10% opacity), with enough contrast to remain visible.
```

## 修改說明 / How to Adapt

- 換對手：蝸牛換成青蛙或甲蟲，SCENE 的黏液光痕改成對應的痕跡（如水珠、螢光粉）。
- 換瓷磚花紋：伊茲尼克鬱金香紋換成青花瓷或馬賽克，STYLE & LIGHT 的紋樣描寫跟著換。
- 換光線：柔和側光換成正午頂光或黃昏暖光，鎧甲反光與影子的描寫跟著改。

- Swap the opponent: replace the snail with a frog or beetle, and change the slime trail in SCENE to a matching trace (water drops, glowing dust).
- Swap the tile pattern: replace the Iznik tulip motif with blue-and-white porcelain or mosaic, updating the pattern description in STYLE & LIGHT.
- Change the light: swap the soft side light for noon top light or dusk warmth, updating the armour reflections and shadows.
