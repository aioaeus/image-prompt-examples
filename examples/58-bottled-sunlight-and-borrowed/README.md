## 修改說明 / How to Adapt

- 換商品：把「瓶裝陽光」換成「瓶裝雨聲」「罐裝新鮮空氣」，SUBJECT & IDEA 的買家反應同步改。
- 換買家：把上班族換成熬夜的學生或加班的護士，時間焦慮的主題不變。
- 改光影：把金黃陽光改成冷藍色「月光」，並把手錶改成沙漏，氛圍完全不同。

- Swap the goods: replace bottled sunlight with bottled rain sounds or canned fresh air, and update the buyers' reactions in SUBJECT & IDEA.
- Change the buyers: swap the businessmen for cramming students or night-shift nurses — the time-anxiety theme stays.
- Reshape the light: trade golden sunlight for cold-blue "moonlight" and the wristwatch for an hourglass for a totally different mood.
